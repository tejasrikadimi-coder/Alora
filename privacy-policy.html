Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Alora Handmade Jewelry ("Alora", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard the information you provide when visiting our website (alora-handmade-jewelry.web.app), creating an account, browsing our collections, or placing orders.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with our handcrafted jewelry and seamless shopping experience:

  • Account Information: When you create an account or sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and optional delivery address.
  • Order & Transaction Information: When you place an order, we collect delivery address details, contact phone number, items ordered, and UPI Transaction Reference (UTR) for payment verification. We do NOT collect or store your UPI PIN, banking passwords, or payment card details.
  • Customer Inquiries & Messages: When you submit a message through our Contact Us form, we collect your name, email, subject, and message content to assist you.
  • Product Reviews: When you review a delivered product, we collect your star rating, review text, customer display name, and optional uploaded product photo.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • Processing, packing, shipping, and delivering your jewelry orders.
  • Sending transactional order confirmations and delivery updates via EmailJS and official communication channels.
  • Providing customer support through email (alorajewels26@gmail.com) and WhatsApp (+91 9392159623).
  • Managing your personalized wishlist and shopping cart across sessions and devices.
  • Displaying genuine customer reviews on product detail pages.
  • Preventing fraudulent orders and maintaining platform security.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate our website securely and reliably, we work with industry-leading cloud service providers:

  • Google Firebase: Provides user authentication, encrypted cloud database storage (Cloud Firestore), and website hosting. Subject to Google's strict privacy and security standards.
  • Cloudinary: Provides secure cloud storage and optimization for product images and customer review photos via unsigned direct uploads.
  • EmailJS: Dispatches transactional order confirmation emails to customers and new-order alerts to store administration.
  • Google Analytics (Firebase): Collects anonymized, aggregated browsing and interaction statistics to improve website usability. No sensitive personal data, passwords, or payment credentials are ever sent to analytics.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Alora uses standard browser localStorage and sessionStorage to preserve your shopping cart, wishlist items, and session preferences while browsing as a guest. When you sign in, your cart and wishlist synchronize securely with your authenticated Firebase user profile.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard security protocols, HTTPS encryption, and Cloud Firestore Security Rules to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your data. We retain order records for accounting, taxation, and shipping fulfillment obligations.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to view, update, or correct your personal profile information at any time via your Profile page. You also have the right to request deletion of your account. Account deletion permanently removes your login credentials and personal profile, while past order records are archived as required by commercial regulations.
