Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with Alora

🛍️ Ordering & Products

How do I place an order on Alora?

Browse our Collections, select your desired jewelry piece, choose your quantity, and click "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now". Review your order details, select your preferred payment method (UPI or Cash on Delivery), and submit your order. You will receive an instant order confirmation and email with your unique order ID.

Can I request custom-made jewelry?

Yes! As an artisanal handmade jewelry studio, we love creating bespoke pieces tailored to your outfit, bridal theme, or personal taste. Please message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 9392159623 or use our Contact Us page with your inspiration images and requirements.

Are your products truly handmade?

Every single piece in our catalog is crafted by hand with careful attention to detail. Because each design is handcrafted, subtle variations in stones, wire-weaving, and finish give each piece its unique artisan charm.

💳 Payments & Verification

How does Online Payment via UPI work?

When you select Online Payment (UPI) at checkout, our system generates a dynamic QR code pre-filled with the exact order total and our merchant UPI ID (9392159623@ybl). Scan the QR code using Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or any bank UPI app to complete payment. Enter the 12-digit UTR / Reference number from your banking app in the checkout form to complete placement.

Why does my order say "Payment Verification Pending"?

To ensure security, all UPI payments are manually reconciled against our bank statement before dispatch. Once verified by our accounts desk, your order status updates to Confirmed.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available for supported pin codes. Select COD at checkout and pay the courier executive upon parcel delivery.

🚚 Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?

In-stock items are dispatched within 2 to 3 business days. Standard courier transit takes 5 to 7 business days across major Indian cities and states. Custom pieces may require 4–7 days crafting time prior to dispatch.

How can I track my order?

Log in to your account and navigate to My Orders. Each active order displays a real-time progress timeline from Order Received through Shipped and Delivered.

🔄 Cancellations & Returns

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, you can request cancellation directly from your My Orders page while the order is still in Order Received or Confirmed status. Once an order enters Processing or Shipped, cancellations cannot be accepted.

What is your Return and Exchange policy?

We offer a 7-day return/exchange window from delivery for items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect. To submit a claim, visit My Orders on the delivered order. An unboxing video is strongly recommended.

✨ Jewelry Care & Maintenance

How should I care for my handmade jewelry?

To preserve your jewelry's beauty and shine:

  • Keep away from water, moisture, sweat, and hand sanitizers.
  • Apply perfumes, body lotions, and hairspray before putting on your jewelry.
  • Store each piece individually in an airtight plastic pouch or soft velvet box.
