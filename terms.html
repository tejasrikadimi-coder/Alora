Customer satisfaction is paramount at Alora. We provide a 7-day return and exchange window from the date of confirmed delivery under the following conditions:

  • An unboxing video showing the sealed parcel being opened is strongly recommended to substantiate transit damage claims.
  • Returned items must be unused, in their original packaging, with tags and tamper seals intact.
  • Customized jewelry made specifically to customer specifications is non-returnable unless defective.

8. Jewelry Care Guidelines

To maintain the luster and longevity of your handcrafted jewelry:

  • Avoid direct contact with water, sweat, perfumes, hairsprays, and cleaning chemicals.
  • Store each piece separately in an airtight ziplock pouch or velvet box away from direct sunlight.
  • Gently wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth after wear before storing.
